## 💡 The Problem
Traditional support systems frustrate users with endless FAQ menus, static chatbots that completely miss context, and the agony of repeating order numbers and bank details across multiple human transfers. Customers wait hours in queue just for a human agent to gather basic details that an intelligent system should have automated upfront.

## ✨ The Solution: Nexura AI
Nexura AI replaces legacy chatbots with a cohesive **10-stage autonomous cycle** that instantly classifies pain points, calculates urgency, generates formal ticket dossiers, and routes cases seamlessly to the right department or human escalation cockpit.

### The 10-Stage Autonomous Pipeline
1. **Customer Message:** Natural language input in any format.
2. **Intent Detection:** Identifies core problem semantics.
3. **Complaint Recognition:** Differentiates casual queries vs. active complaints.
4. **Category Classification:** Sorts into 10 enterprise-grade categories.
5. **Priority Detection:** Calculates severity from Low to Critical.
6. **Entity Extraction:** Pulls monetary amounts, order IDs, and dates.
7. **Ticket Generation:** Instantly generates a unique ticket ID (e.g., `CS-2026-10482`).
8. **Department Routing:** Dispatches to Finance, Tech, Logistics, etc.
9. **Smart Escalation:** Hands off critical cases to human agents.
10. **Feedback Loop:** Captures CSAT ratings and resolution metrics.
